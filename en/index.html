Physiology & experience

How mild HBOT (mHBOT) works in the body

Normally, oxygen is carried almost exclusively by red blood cells. When you breathe under mildly increased pressure (up to 1.5 ATA), oxygen dissolves directly into your body's fluids – including blood plasma, lymph, and cerebrospinal fluid.

This allows oxygen to reach areas and tissues with reduced microcirculation, calm inflammation, and support your body's natural healing processes.

What does a session feel like?

As pressure gently rises, you will feel it in your ears – just like during airplane takeoff. You easily equalize by swallowing or yawning. Within a few minutes the sensation fades, and you can rest, nap, or read a physical book in complete peace.

Technical checklist

Practical home requirements

  • Floor space & weight: Min. 2.5 × 2.0 meters of clear floor area (chamber approx. 2.35 × 1.20 m). Total weight approx. 120 kg on a solid, flat surface.
  • Power & grounding: 220–230V outlet with active grounding (strict safety requirement) and 3 free sockets. Continuous draw approx. 1,200W – recommended on a 10A or 13A circuit without heavy appliances running simultaneously.
  • Temperature: Room temperature between 10 °C and 28 °C ensures optimal operation of the compressor and concentrator.
  • Ventilation: Ability to open a window or use active ventilation/AC. Avoid rooms with strong cooking odors.
  • Technical service: RechargeLife handles all filter changes, sanitization, and maintenance between rentals.
Uncompromising safety

6 core safety standards

  • 1. Intro video & safety quiz: Short online module covering safe operation and ear equalization, completed by all users before starting.
  • 2. Certified setup: Our technician assembles, tests, and walks you through zippers, valves, and the timer in person.
  • 3. Supervised first session: Your first practical run is carried out together with us – at home or in our clinic.
  • 4. No overnight stays: Max 360 oxygen units/day (up to 4 hours at 1.5 ATA). You may use the timer app, so you are able to keep an eye on air-breaks and time using your tablet or phone outside the chamber.
  • 5. Proper mask: We recommend a non-rebreathing mask with one-way valves (standard oxygen masks/cannulas also work). Always inspect before use.
  • 6. Antistatic attire: Wear 100% cotton, linen, or bamboo inside the chamber – avoid synthetic fabrics like nylon and polyester.
Danish Expertise & FAQ

Frequently asked questions

RecoveryExpressen is operated by RechargeLife in Hillerød – backed by an M.Sc. in Engineering (DTU), IBUM certification as Advanced Hyperbaric Technician & Safety Director, and EBAss membership.

What is the difference between home rental and the clinic in Hillerød? +

At our clinic in Hillerød, we operate at up to 2.0 ATA in PED-approved hard-shell chambers with an affiliated physician for intensive protocols. Home rental of soft-shell chambers (max 1.5 ATA) is a flexible wellness and recovery solution for frequent, daily sessions without travel time.

Do I need a medical exam before renting a chamber? +

No. Because mild HBOT is a gentle wellness routine at low pressure, a medical exam is not required. If you want individual medical guidance before starting, you can easily book an optional consultation via rechargelife.dk.

Am I left on my own once the chamber is installed at home? +

Not at all. Your rental includes 3 scheduled monthly follow-ups via email or phone where we review your experience and fine-tune your protocol. Additional coaching or a physician consultation can always be added.

Why am I not allowed to sleep overnight in the chamber? +

When breathing concentrated oxygen under pressure, your body accumulates oxygen units (OTU). For a healthy, safe balance, you should not exceed 360 OTU/day (max 4 hours at 1.5 ATA). Use our free timer app on a phone, tablet, or computer placed outside the chamber window: Open HBOT Session Timer →

Which oxygen mask is best to use? +

We recommend a non-rebreathing mask with a reservoir bag and one-way valves for the purest oxygen delivery without rebreathing exhaled air. Standard oxygen masks and nasal cannulas also work well. Extra masks are always available from us.
